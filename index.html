How it works

Three simple steps from registration to riding.

1

Register

Open Register, choose Customer, Seller or Vendor, and fill in your details. Sellers and Vendors wait as Pending for Admin approval.

2

Log in

Use the single login page. MotoManage opens your role-specific dashboard automatically based on your account.

3

Buy, Sell or Manage

Book test rides, compare motorcycles, process showroom sales, or fulfill vendor purchase orders right from your dashboard.
